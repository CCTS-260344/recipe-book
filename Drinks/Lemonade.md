## Recipe for Lemonade ##
## Ingredients##
- A jar 
- A bottle of water
## Instructions ##
- Drink you water
- Wait until you can tell the lemonade is ready (**Make sure your partially dehydrated**) 
- Get your jar
- Take a whizz in it
- Enjoy the Lemonade


## **Secret Bonus Point Oppurtunity** ##
- Share your lemonade with a friend instead of enjoying it to yourself.
